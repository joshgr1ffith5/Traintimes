Chislehurst board settings

Departure station and display
Selecting a station shows trains to every destination; Automatic keeps your original commute view. Classic mode shows three trains with quicker, complete calling-point scrolling. For other stations, enter their three-letter National Rail code.
Announcements
Scheduled mode only shows announcement pages in the chosen daily window. A window crossing midnight is supported. Select every day or weekdays; if neither is selected, announcements stay off.
Spoken announcements
Celia ONLY uses recorded clips for platform, departure time, operator, destination, calling points and carriage count where available; no Alexa voice is used. Choose Always on for announcements after 22:00.
Experimental Echo Show keep-open
Unverified workaround, not an Amazon-supported kiosk feature. It may not stop Silk from returning home. Requires an initial tap and may stop after a reboot.
Custom announcement
Optional inclusive dates. Custom messages are yours, not verified railway notices.
Southeastern official updates
Follow live messages from Southeastern Control. WhatsApp and X do not provide an approved browser-readable feed here; these links open the official channels. Only confirmed Darwin changes automatically appear on this board.

Night display
Untick to keep the board at full brightness all night. This controls the webpage only, not Alexa’s own night mode.
Appearance & testing

Open this menu: tap the invisible top-left corner once, or press M on a keyboard. Train times and station messages come from the official National Rail detailed departures API via your Cloudflare Worker; engineering notices are retrieved through your Cloudflare Worker. Only incidents identified as planned engineering appear on the engineering screen. When none are returned the screen is skipped. Preview engineering screen uses an unmistakably labelled example and does not represent live railway information.
